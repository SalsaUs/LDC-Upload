Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a land survey cost in Victoria?

Cost depends on site size, complexity and survey type. Describe your project and professionals can quote.

Do I need a surveyor to subdivide?

Yes. A licensed surveyor prepares the plan of subdivision and lodges it for title.

What is the difference between a surveyor and an engineer?

Surveyors measure and define land. Engineers design infrastructure and structures.

Can you help if my neighbour disputes the boundary?

A re-establishment survey is the usual first step.

Do you service regional Victoria?

Yes, across Victoria.

How quickly will I hear back?

Submit an enquiry and we aim to respond promptly with next steps.

Do I need a surveyor, engineer or planner?

Tell us your goal. Surveyors measure and define land, engineers design, and planners handle approvals. We point you to the right discipline.

Is it free to enquire?

Yes. Enquiries are free and carry no obligation.

Are the professionals licensed?

We only introduce licensed Victorian surveyors and engineers.
