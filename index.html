Who we are

15+ years across surveying and engineering.

We have worked on both sides of the land development process, in surveying and in civil engineering, across projects from single-lot title fixes to staged subdivisions.

That experience taught us one thing above all: the right licensed professional matters. In Australia, cadastral surveying and engineering sign-off are regulated for good reason. The wrong person can cost you months, a failed council or title submission, or a boundary you have to redo.

So we do not guess. We ask the right questions up front and point you to someone qualified for that specific job.
