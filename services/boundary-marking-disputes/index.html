Boundary survey and title re-establishment

A re-establishment survey locates your legal boundaries from title and survey records and marks them on the ground, helping before you build a fence, or when neighbours disagree.

What is included

  • Boundary pegging and re-establishment
  • Fence and encroachment checks
  • Identification surveys for building
  • Evidence reports for boundary disputes

Why use a licensed professional?

In Australia, cadastral surveying and engineering sign-off are regulated. Using the wrong person can mean failed council or title submissions and costly rework. We match you with someone qualified for this specific job.
