Geotechnical engineers and soil testing

Geotechnical testing identifies soil type, reactivity and bearing capacity to guide foundations and retaining walls.

What is included

  • Soil classification
  • Bearing capacity
  • Slope stability
  • Reports for building permits

Why use a licensed professional?

In Australia, cadastral surveying and engineering sign-off are regulated. Using the wrong person can mean failed council or title submissions and costly rework. We match you with someone qualified for this specific job.
