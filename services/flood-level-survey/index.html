Flood level surveys for building permits

Many Victorian sites need a flood level survey to confirm floor levels relative to council or authority flood levels.

What is included

  • Ground and floor level certification
  • Relating levels to flood data
  • Support for permit applications
  • Existing building level checks

Why use a licensed professional?

In Australia, cadastral surveying and engineering sign-off are regulated. Using the wrong person can mean failed council or title submissions and costly rework. We match you with someone qualified for this specific job.
