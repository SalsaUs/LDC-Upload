Town planners and planning permits Victoria

Town planners assess your site against the planning scheme and prepare permit applications for subdivision, development and use.

What is included

  • Planning feasibility advice
  • Permit applications
  • Overlay and zoning review
  • VCAT and objection support

Why use a licensed professional?

In Australia, cadastral surveying and engineering sign-off are regulated. Using the wrong person can mean failed council or title submissions and costly rework. We match you with someone qualified for this specific job.
